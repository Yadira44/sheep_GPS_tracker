# sheep_GPS_tracker
The GPS tracker will track a sheep's location throughout the day and monitor how they traverse 10 acres of land. 
The most/least visited spots of the sheep can help me determine if grass there is more ideal for the sheep, or if the locations depend on their accessible water sources. The temperature on a daily basis will also be taken into consideration in this study to determine if it is another entity that affects the sheep’s grazing. 


## FYI's 
- The GPS tracker will be made into a collar and will be intended to be worn during the day only. (Time's : TBD)
- The temperature will not be measured from the tracker itself, but done by myself.
- The data from the GPS tracker will be layered with a map of the 10 acres of land that include; trees, water sources, houses, etc.
